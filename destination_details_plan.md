## Photo Acquisition Plan

The objective is to find high-quality, open-licensed, or public-domain photos representing each location. These photos should cover:
1. **Birds**: Key avian species unique to or famous in the area.
2. **Wildlife**: Core mammals, reptiles, or amphibians found in the habitat.
3. **General Location / Landscape**: Landscape views, forests, habitats, or key geographic features that define the region.

### Intended Sources
- **Wikimedia Commons**: High-quality creative commons photography of landscapes and specific wildlife/bird species.
- **iNaturalist / Flickr**: Excellent sources for specific biodiversity photographs under Creative Commons licenses.
- **Unsplash / Pexels / Pixabay**: High-quality general landscape and common wildlife photos.

### Proposed Details/Target Species per Location

#### 1. Agumbe, Someshwara & Kudremukh Belt (Karnataka)
* **Birds**: Malabar Trogon, Sri Lanka Frogmouth, Malabar Whistling Thrush.
* **Wildlife**: King Cobra, Lion-tailed Macaque, Malabar Giant Squirrel.
* **Landscape**: Dense rainforest canopy, wet evergreen forest ghats, waterfalls (Barkana/Onake Abbi).

#### 2. Bandhavgarh National Park (Madhya Pradesh)
* **Birds**: Lesser Adjutant, Malabar Pied Hornbill, Mottled Wood Owl.
* **Wildlife**: Bengal Tiger, Leopard, Dhole (Asiatic Wild Dog), Gaur.
* **Landscape**: Sal forests, rocky hills with ancient fort ruins, grasslands.

#### 3. Bandipur & Mudumalai Belt (Karnataka / Tamil Nadu)
* **Birds**: Indian Vulture, Malabar Grey Hornbill, Crested Serpent Eagle.
* **Wildlife**: Indian Elephant, Bengal Tiger, Gaur, Sloth Bear.
* **Landscape**: Dry deciduous forests, Moyar gorge, Nilgiri hills backdrop.

#### 4. Banni & Greater Rann of Kutch (Gujarat)
* **Birds**: Macqueen's Bustard, Greater Flamingo, Sociable Lapwing, Steppe Eagle.
* **Wildlife**: Desert Fox, Indian Wild Ass, Golden Jackal.
* **Landscape**: Salt marsh flats (White Rann), dry grasslands (Banni).

#### 5. Bhadra Tiger Reserve (Karnataka)
* **Birds**: Great Hornbill, Emerald Dove, Malabar Parakeet.
* **Wildlife**: Tiger, Leopard, Sloth Bear, Gaur.
* **Landscape**: Bhadra reservoir, teak/bamboo forests, Kemmangundi hills backdrop.

#### 6. Chopta, Mandal & Munsiyari (Uttarakhand)
* **Birds**: Himalayan Monal, Koklass Pheasant, Cheer Pheasant, Fire-fronted Serin.
* **Wildlife**: Himalayan Musk Deer, Red Fox, Yellow-throated Marten.
* **Landscape**: Snow-clad Himalayan peaks (Panchachuli, Trishul), alpine meadows (bugyals), rhododendron forests.

#### 7. Dandeli & Kali Tiger Reserve (Karnataka)
* **Birds**: Great Hornbill, Malabar Pied Hornbill, Heart-spotted Woodpecker.
* **Wildlife**: Black Panther (melanistic Leopard), Sloth Bear, Malabar Giant Squirrel.
* **Landscape**: Kali River rapids, dense jungle, limestone caves.

#### 8. Desert National Park (Rajasthan)
* **Birds**: Great Indian Bustard, Cream-colored Courser, Lagger Falcon.
* **Wildlife**: Chinkara, Desert Cat, Sandfish (skink).
* **Landscape**: Sand dunes, dry scrub, fossil beds.

#### 9. Eaglenest Wildlife Sanctuary (Arunachal Pradesh)
* **Birds**: Bugun Liocichla, Ward's Trogon, Beautiful Nuthatch, Fire-tailed Myzornis.
* **Wildlife**: Red Panda, Bhutan Glory (butterfly), Golden Cat.
* **Landscape**: Cloud forests, bamboo thickets, Eastern Himalayan foothills.

#### 10. Gir National Park (Gujarat)
* **Birds**: Mottled Wood Owl, Painted Sandgrouse, Asian Paradise Flycatcher.
* **Wildlife**: Asiatic Lion, Striped Hyena, Chousingha (Four-horned Antelope).
* **Landscape**: Teak forest, dry deciduous scrub, Kamleshwar dam reservoir.

#### 11. Great Himalayan National Park (Himachal Pradesh)
* **Birds**: Western Tragopan, Cheer Pheasant, Himalayan Monal.
* **Wildlife**: Snow Leopard, Himalayan Blue Sheep (Bharal), Himalayan Tahr.
* **Landscape**: Alpine meadows, glaciers, pine and oak forests.

#### 12. Hampi & Daroji Bear Sanctuary Region (Karnataka)
* **Birds**: Painted Spurfowl, Yellow-throated Bulbul, Indian Eagle-Owl.
* **Wildlife**: Sloth Bear, Indian Star Tortoise, Ruddy Mongoose.
* **Landscape**: Boulder-strewn terrain, ancient temple ruins, scrub forests.

#### 13. Hemis National Park (Ladakh)
* **Birds**: Golden Eagle, Lammergeier (Bearded Vulture), Himalayan Snowcock.
* **Wildlife**: Snow Leopard, Tibetan Argali, Bharal, Eurasian Lynx.
* **Landscape**: Cold desert valleys, snowy peaks, rugged rocky gorges.

#### 14. Kabini / Nagarhole (Karnataka)
* **Birds**: Malabar Pied Hornbill, Crested Hawk-Eagle, Osprey.
* **Wildlife**: Tiger, Leopard (including the famous melanistic leopard "Saya"), Asiatic Elephant, Gaur.
* **Landscape**: Kabini River/backwaters, lush teak forests, open swamps (hadlus).

#### 15. Kanha National Park (Madhya Pradesh)
* **Birds**: Indian Roller, Malabar Pied Hornbill, Orange-headed Thrush.
* **Wildlife**: Bengal Tiger, Barasingha (Swamp Deer), Dhole, Leopard.
* **Landscape**: Sal forests, expansive meadows, Bamni Dadar (sunset point).

#### 16. Kaziranga National Park (Assam)
* **Birds**: Bengal Florican, Great Hornbill, Swamp Francolin.
* **Wildlife**: Greater One-horned Rhinoceros, Wild Water Buffalo, Eastern Swamp Deer, Elephant.
* **Landscape**: Tall elephant grass, swampy lagoons (beels), Brahmaputra floodplain.

#### 17. Keoladeo National Park / Bharatpur (Rajasthan)
* **Birds**: Siberian Crane (historical), Sarus Crane, Painted Stork, Bar-headed Goose.
* **Wildlife**: Nilgai, Golden Jackal, Rock Python.
* **Landscape**: Wetlands, shallow marshes, woodland-edge savannahs.

#### 18. Kumarakom & Vembanad Lake (Kerala)
* **Birds**: Watercock, Siberian Crane (migrant), Little Cormorant, White-breasted Waterhen.
* **Wildlife**: Indian Otter, diverse freshwater fish species.
* **Landscape**: Vembanad Lake, mangrove forests, coconut groves, houseboats.

#### 19. Little Rann of Kutch (Gujarat)
* **Birds**: Lesser Flamingo, Peregrine Falcon, Houbara Bustard.
* **Wildlife**: Indian Wild Ass (Khur), Desert Fox, Nilgai.
* **Landscape**: Flat salt-encrusted cracked mudflats, scrubby islands (bets).

#### 20. Mangalajodi / Chilika Lake (Odisha)
* **Birds**: Black-tailed Godwit, Northern Pintail, Peregrine Falcon, Purple Heron.
* **Wildlife**: Irrawaddy Dolphin (in Chilika deep water), Fishing Cat.
* **Landscape**: Vast wetland marshes, reed beds, blue water channels.

#### 21. Mishmi Hills & Dibang Valley (Arunachal Pradesh)
* **Birds**: Mishmi Wren-Babbler, Sclater's Monal, Blyth's Tragopan.
* **Wildlife**: Mishmi Takin, Red Panda, Musk Deer.
* **Landscape**: Mist-shrouded temperate forests, deep valleys, fast-flowing rivers.

#### 22. Munnar, Eravikulam & Chinnar (Kerala)
* **Birds**: Nilgiri Pipit, Nilgiri Wood Pigeon, Broad-tailed Grassbird.
* **Wildlife**: Nilgiri Tahr, Grizzled Giant Squirrel, Nilgiri Langur.
* **Landscape**: Tea gardens, Anamudi peak (highest in South India), shola grassland ecosystems.

#### 23. Namdapha National Park (Arunachal Pradesh)
* **Birds**: Snowy-throated Babbler, Rufous-necked Hornbill, White-winged Duck.
* **Wildlife**: Namdapha Flying Squirrel, Clouded Leopard, Hoolock Gibbon.
* **Landscape**: Subtropical rainforests, Noa-Dihing River, snowcapped peaks.

#### 24. Pangot, Sattal & Corbett (Uttarakhand)
* **Birds**: Cheer Pheasant, Koklass Pheasant, Crested Kingfisher, Great Hornbill.
* **Wildlife**: Bengal Tiger, Elephant, Leopard, Gharial.
* **Landscape**: Oak and pine forests (Pangot/Sattal), Ramganga River, grasslands (Chaurs of Corbett).

#### 25. Panna National Park (Madhya Pradesh)
* **Birds**: King Vulture, Painted Sandgrouse, Indian Scops Owl.
* **Wildlife**: Bengal Tiger, Leopard, Sloth Bear, Gharial.
* **Landscape**: Ken River gorge, dry deciduous forests, rocky escarpments.

#### 26. Pench National Park (MP / Maharashtra)
* **Birds**: Malabar Pied Hornbill, Indian Pitta, Collared Scops Owl.
* **Wildlife**: Tiger, Leopard, Dhole, Chital.
* **Landscape**: Teak forest canopy, Pench River, open grassy meadows.

#### 27. Periyar National Park (Kerala)
* **Birds**: Malabar Grey Hornbill, Nilgiri Wood Pigeon, Great Hornbill.
* **Wildlife**: Elephant, Tiger, Nilgiri Langur, Lion-tailed Macaque.
* **Landscape**: Periyar Lake reservoir, evergreen rainforests, grassy hills.

#### 28. Ranganathittu Bird Sanctuary (Karnataka)
* **Birds**: Painted Stork, Asian Openbill Stork, Eurasian Spoonbill, Streak-throated Swallow.
* **Wildlife**: Marsh Crocodile (Mugger), Flying Fox (Fruit bats).
* **Landscape**: Cauvery River islets, rocky riverine habitat.

#### 29. Ranthambore National Park (Rajasthan)
* **Birds**: Painted Sandgrouse, Indian Courser, Crested Serpent Eagle.
* **Wildlife**: Bengal Tiger, Leopard, Caracal, Sloth Bear.
* **Landscape**: Dry deciduous forests, lakes (Padam Talao), historical Ranthambore Fort ruins.

#### 30. Satpura National Park (Madhya Pradesh)
* **Birds**: Malabar Whistling Thrush, Indian Skimmer, Grey Hornbill.
* **Wildlife**: Sloth Bear, Leopard, Tiger, Indian Giant Squirrel.
* **Landscape**: Sandstone peaks (Pachmarhi), deep gorges, Denwa river/reservoir.

#### 31. Sundarbans National Park (West Bengal)
* **Birds**: Brown-winged Kingfisher, Masked Finfoot, White-bellied Sea Eagle.
* **Wildlife**: Bengal Tiger (famous swimming tigers), Estuarine Crocodile, River Terrapin.
* **Landscape**: Dense mangrove forests, mudflats, tidal rivers and creeks.

#### 32. Tadoba-Andhari Tiger Reserve (Maharashtra)
* **Birds**: Grey-headed Fish Eagle, Mottled Wood Owl, Crested Honey Buzzard.
* **Wildlife**: Bengal Tiger, Sloth Bear, Leopard, Dhole.
* **Landscape**: Teak and bamboo forests, Tadoba Lake, rugged cliffs.

#### 33. Tal Chhapar Sanctuary (Rajasthan)
* **Birds**: Demoiselle Crane, Laggar Falcon, Montagu's Harrier.
* **Wildlife**: Blackbuck, Desert Cat, Desert Fox.
* **Landscape**: Flat salt clay loam grassland, acacia scrub.

#### 34. Thattekad Bird Sanctuary (Kerala)
* **Birds**: Sri Lanka Frogmouth, Malabar Trogon, Indian Pitta, Jerdon's Nightjar.
* **Wildlife**: Sloth Bear, Leopard (rarely seen), Flying Lizard.
* **Landscape**: Lowland evergreen forest, Periyar River branch.

#### 35. Valparai & Anamalai Tiger Reserve (Tamil Nadu)
* **Birds**: Great Hornbill, Nilgiri Wood Pigeon, Malabar Trogon.
* **Wildlife**: Lion-tailed Macaque, Nilgiri Tahr, Elephant, Gaur.
* **Landscape**: Tea/coffee plantations, rainforest fragments, Anamalai hills.

#### 36. Vedanthangal & Pulicat Lake (Tamil Nadu)
* **Birds**: Greater Flamingo, Spot-billed Pelican, Painted Stork, Glossy Ibis.
* **Wildlife**: Jungle Cat, diverse estuarine fish and crabs.
* **Landscape**: Traditional nesting grove pond (Vedanthangal), vast brackish water lagoon (Pulicat).

#### 37. Velavadar Blackbuck National Park (Gujarat)
* **Birds**: Montagu's Harrier, Pallid Harrier, Lesser Florican, Saras Crane.
* **Wildlife**: Blackbuck, Indian Wolf, Striped Hyena.
* **Landscape**: Vast golden grasslands, Gulf of Khambhat mudflats.

#### 38. Wayanad Wildlife Sanctuary (Kerala)
* **Birds**: Malabar Grey Hornbill, Blue-winged Parakeet, Rufous Babbler.
* **Wildlife**: Elephant, Tiger, Gaur, Bonnet Macaque.
* **Landscape**: Moist deciduous teak forests, bamboo glades, Western Ghats vistas.
