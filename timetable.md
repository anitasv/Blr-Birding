# India Birding, Wildlife, and Photography Timetable

**Base:** Bengaluru, India  
**Period:** October 2026 to December 2029  
**Selection rule:** For each month, greedily pick one unselected destination only if that month falls within that destination’s own best time to visit. If no strong unselected destination qualifies, mark the month as skipped.

## Notes

- This is optimized for seasonal fit, not equal geographic distribution.
- Winter months are overloaded because India’s best birding and comfortable wildlife travel are heavily concentrated from November to February.
- March to May is often best for tiger and mammal photography, but heat can be severe.
- Monsoon months are mostly skipped except where monsoon itself improves the photographic value, such as Agumbe.
