## Notes

- For most Indian wildlife parks, **October–March** is the comfortable season.
- **March–May** is often better for mammal sightings near water, but it can be very hot.
- Winter wetlands are usually best from **November–February**.
- For remote Northeast India destinations, guides, permits, and logistics matter more than raw distance.
- For serious wildlife photography, book safaris and zones early, especially for Kabini, Tadoba, Bandhavgarh, Kanha, Ranthambore, and Gir.
